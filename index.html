HEADBIRDS / TALKS

Talks

Ideas are meant to be shared.

TALKS · WORKSHOPS · MASTERCLASSES · ARTISTIC MENTORING

For educational institutions, cultural spaces, festivals, creative organisations and individual artists.

Teaching, exchanging ideas and sharing knowledge have always been part of the creative journey.

From DJ courses and sound design workshops to children's music workshops, live performance masterclasses and conversations about club culture and the electronic music scene, these experiences have created opportunities to explore music from different perspectives.

Collaborations with institutions such as EUMES Girona, The Bass Valley and Escola de Música de Banyoles have brought together technical knowledge, artistic curiosity and a shared desire to experiment.

Artistic mentoring is another essential part of this practice. Closely connected to music production for other artists, it focuses on accompanying the creative process, exploring ideas and developing a personal musical identity. Rather than offering predefined solutions, the aim is to listen, question, experiment and help each artist find their own voice.

Beyond teaching techniques or sharing experience, the intention is to create meaningful exchanges where knowledge, creativity and different perspectives can meet.

HEADBIRDS / 01

About

An ongoing practice in electronic music.

For around two decades, HEADBIRDS has developed a sound that resists easy categorisation. House provides the warmth, electro introduces syncopation, and techno gives the music its physical force. The result moves between genres without settling into one.

Across EPs and albums including Kulturhaus and Atzar, that approach has travelled through clubs and festivals from Barcelona and Paris to New York, Hong Kong and Bangkok.

But the work extends beyond records and DJ sets. Creating spaces for music and for other artists — including Les Arcs, L’Estrany and COREA, among other projects — belongs to the same restless artistic impulse.
